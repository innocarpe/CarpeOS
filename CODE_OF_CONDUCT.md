# Code of Conduct

CarpeOS contributors are expected to keep the project professional, respectful,
and technically grounded.

## Expected Behavior

- Be direct and respectful.
- Critique ideas, code, and evidence rather than people.
- Keep discussions focused on the project.
- Respect privacy boundaries.
- Use synthetic examples when discussing bugs or design.
- Credit third-party work accurately.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, threats, or personal attacks.
- Publishing private user data, credentials, logs, or session transcripts.
- Pressuring contributors to disclose private runtime state.
- Misrepresenting planned work as completed work.
- Removing third-party license notices from reused material.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove content, close issues, reject pull requests, or restrict
participation when behavior harms the project or its contributors.

Security-sensitive or privacy-sensitive issues should be handled through the
process in `SECURITY.md`.
